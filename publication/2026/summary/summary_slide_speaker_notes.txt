The strongest claim is within the 2026 survey. Among current users with complete answers on use, attitude and trust, 520 of 958 very unfavorable respondents reported using at least one AI tool type daily. That is 54%, compared with 93% of very favorable users. The attitude gap remains substantial, but frequent use and dislike clearly coexist. The study models also found that attitude discriminated daily from less frequent use better than the trust item. These are associations from a voluntary, self-reported survey. The survey changed its use, attitude and trust questions in 2026, so the 2025-to-2026 differences are not clean trend estimates. The practical recommendation is to measure attitude, reliance and actual work outcomes alongside usage.
