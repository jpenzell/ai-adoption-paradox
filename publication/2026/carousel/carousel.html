Reading the evidence

What the survey can tell us.

A useful warning about interpreting adoption metrics, with limits.

Observed:
Some respondents reported frequent use and a negative attitude at the same time. Attitude and trust provided different predictive signals.
Not established:
Whether attitude causes use, whether policy improves work, or whether the same people became more skeptical over time.
Keep the population in view.
This is a voluntary survey, not a representative sample of all developers or all workers. Use, attitude and trust are self-reported.
Secondary analysis of public Stack Overflow Developer Survey data.
Within-year associations; no causal or population-wide estimate.7 / 9
For an AI rollout

Track use.
Ask what use means.

Frequency is one measure. Add questions about experience, reliance and results.

1

Attitude

How do people feel about using these tools in their actual work?

2

Reliance and verification

Which tasks will they trust AI with? What do they check before using its output?

3

Work outcomes

Does the workflow improve quality, judgment or useful output? Measure that separately.

Practical recommendations from the findings, not tested interventions.
A usage count alone cannot answer these questions.8 / 9
