Reading the evidence

What the survey can tell us.

A useful warning about interpreting adoption metrics, with limits.

Observed:
Some respondents reported frequent use and a negative attitude at the same time. Attitude and trust provided different predictive signals.
Not established:
Whether attitude causes use, whether policy improves work, or whether the same people became more skeptical over time.
Keep the population in view.
This is a voluntary survey, not a representative sample of all developers or all workers. Use, attitude and trust are self-reported.
